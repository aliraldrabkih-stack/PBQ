    (B) Fine-tuning always deletes the model entirely

Q3. What does dataset validation aim to catch?
    (A) Slow network connections
    (B) Poisoned or manipulated samples before they are used in training
    (C) Grammatical errors in the model's output
    (D) Billing anomalies in cloud usage

Q4. What is model drift?
    (A) A model's behaviour gradually diverging from its intended baseline over time
    (B) A physical relocation of model training servers
    (C) A type of encryption algorithm
    (D) A change in the user interface colour scheme
