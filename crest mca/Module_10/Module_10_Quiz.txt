    (B) Systematically probing a model with varied and adversarial inputs to assess reliability under pressure

    (A) Only a list of tools used during testing
    (B) Vulnerability, evidence, business impact, risk rating, and realistic mitigation

Q4. What risk does confidence threshold abuse introduce in an AI identity-verification system?
    (A) Crafted input pushed past the threshold can force an incorrect approval decision
    (B) It always causes the system to reject all users
    (C) It has no effect on identity verification accuracy
    (D) It only affects the system's response time
